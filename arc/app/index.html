What this is

Cosmos Pay is a Stellar wallet for Latin America: a phone number works as the account, local currency comes in by bank transfer and goes out through anchors. A payment intent is the object a merchant charges with. This is that same object on Arc, so the rail underneath can change without the merchant noticing. The contract holds no funds: pay moves USDC straight from the payer to the merchant.
